# Anjana Ashok - Postdoctoral Fellow

Anjana is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the group, where she thinks mostly about continuous gravitational waves and noise processes in Pulsar Timing Arrays. She did her doctoral research at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics in Hannover, on continuous gravitational waves in the context of LIGO detectors.
Anjana’s research interests include method development and deployment for analysis of gravitational wave data. When not engaged in research, she enjoys reading all kinds of books, writing and just being.

## Education

B.Sc Physics, University of Calicut, Kerala, India (2015)
M.Sc Physics, National Institute of Technology, Karnataka, India (2017)
PhD, Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics, Hannover, Germany (2023)

## Research Interests

Gravitational Wave Astronomy, Pulsars as sources and detectors of Gravitational waves
